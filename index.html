3 Permitted envelope

How it is drawn

Drawn as soon as the site is in: the yard lines with dimension strings outside the lot (values in mm), the envelope outline and the envelope itself, from the detected street edge. Nothing crosses the massing.

What is checked
Width, depth, storeys and height against the envelope; the unit count this site's band allows; floor area against FSR 1.00; and, for a design with an accessory dwelling unit behind it, the courtyard rules (3.1.2.4(a), 3.1.2.8(a), 3.1.2.11(c), 3.1.2.5(a)). Options that do not fit say which rule they fail.
