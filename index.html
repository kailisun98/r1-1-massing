3 Permitted envelope

How it is drawn

Drawn as soon as the site is in: the yard lines with dimension strings outside the lot (values in mm), the envelope outline and the envelope itself, from the street side. Nothing crosses the massing.

4 Massing

Pick a form above. Its units, tenure and bedroom mix appear here.

Units

Tenure sets the unit limit (6, or 8 when all rental) and the minimum of family units. The ground floor may hold a shared room, a grocery or a day care instead of a dwelling. Access is how the upper units are reached.

Bedroom mix 3-bed from 105 m², 2-bed from 65, studio on one level; the rest are 1-beds

The British Columbia designs of CMHC's Housing Design Catalogue, tested against this envelope. Click a fitting option to draw it.

What is checked

Width, depth, storeys and height against the envelope; the unit count this site's band allows; floor area against FSR 1.00; and, for a design with an accessory dwelling unit behind it, the courtyard rules (3.1.2.4(a), 3.1.2.8(a), 3.1.2.11(c), 3.1.2.5(a)). Options that do not fit say which rule they fail.
